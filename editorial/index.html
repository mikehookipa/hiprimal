FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Short answers, no fluff. If yours isn’t here, email hello@hiprimal.com — a person reads every message.

How do I take it?

Two capsules, once a day, with food and water. Most women take both with breakfast. One pouch holds 60 capsules — a 30-day supply.

How big are the capsules?

Standard supplement size — not horse pills. Freeze-drying concentrates the organs, so the daily dose stays at two capsules instead of the six or more tablets some brands require.

Will I taste it? Any beef burps?

No. The organs are freeze-dried to a fine powder and sealed in a flavorless gelatin capsule. Nothing touches your taste buds, and taken with food, repeating is rare. If you have a sensitive stomach, take it with your first meal rather than on an empty stomach.

Who should talk to a doctor first?

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive, check with your doctor before taking HiPrimal — liver is high in preformed vitamin A, which has an upper limit in pregnancy. The same goes if you take prescription medication or manage a medical condition.

What if I have high iron or hemochromatosis?

Liver and spleen are rich in heme iron, which is the point for most women — but not if your body stores too much. If you have hemochromatosis or elevated ferritin, don’t take HiPrimal without your doctor’s sign-off.

When will I notice anything?

It varies. Some women report steadier energy within a couple of weeks; nutrient stores take longer to rebuild. Give it a full pouch before judging it — and if you still feel nothing by day 60, use the guarantee.
